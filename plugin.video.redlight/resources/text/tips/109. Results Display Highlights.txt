
Red Light allows you to choose how colours will be used to distinguish between scraper results.

Go to Settings > Results > Results Colour Highlights and choose firstly your desired setting for "Highlight Results Based On".
The 3 choices are:
* PROVIDER - Highlight results based on the provider the result is for i.e. one colour for EasyNews, one colour for Real-debrid etc.
* QUALITY - Highlight results based on the quality of the result i.e. one colour for 4K, one colour for 1080P etc.
* SINGLE COLOUR - Highlight results using one single colour for all result types.

As you change the setting above, relevant colour settings will be shown for you to set as desired.
